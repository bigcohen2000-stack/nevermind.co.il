- **Working title** — Hebrew, dry, provisional. Final title set at writing time.
- **Audience phrase / pain** — the real felt phrase behind it (research-attested
  where possible; `AUDIENCE_LANGUAGE_REPORT §2`).
- **Angle** — the one logical move the article makes (fact/story, mechanism/emotion,
  structure/drama).
- **Free / Members** — planning tag for the future gate. **Premium is not active
  yet** (see `/members`), so "Members" = *planned* deeper content, nothing gated now.
- **Legacy SEO** — maps to a real old-site URL from the `CLIENT_SOURCE.md` 301
  table; writing these first preserves domain authority on migration.

**Free vs Members principle (from CLIENT_SOURCE / PremiumGate):** entry-level,
foundational pieces are **Free**; the full deep breakdown of a mechanism is
**Members**. Every article shows a free summary regardless.
